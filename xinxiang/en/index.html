Taiwan Xinxiang Association

Let Art Become a Conversation with Yourself.

Taiwan Xinxiang Association is a Taiwanese non-profit that uses painting, colour and art appreciation to invite people to notice their feelings and reconnect with themselves, with others and with the world.

The full English website is in preparation. The English name of the association is provisional and pending confirmation.
